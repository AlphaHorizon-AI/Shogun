# Privacy

Shogun is self-hosted and does not silently inspect an operator's work.
Installation telemetry is optional, disabled by default, and limited to the
documented adoption schema in [telemetry.md](telemetry.md).

Consent is the version-one legal basis proposed by the product architecture.
Consent must be freely given, specific, informed, affirmative, versioned, and as
easy to withdraw as to provide. Declining or withdrawing does not affect Shogun
features, security, updates, model access, or support eligibility.

Before production launch Alpha Horizon must complete a focused Danish/EU privacy
review, decide whether a DPIA is required, maintain processing and processor
records, execute processor agreements, approve retention and incident procedures,
and publish:

<https://www.alphahorizon.io/shogun/telemetry-privacy/>

The published notice must identify the controller and contact route, purposes,
data fields, excluded content, legal basis, EU hosting and processors, retention,
withdrawal, deletion, data-subject rights, and notice version history.
